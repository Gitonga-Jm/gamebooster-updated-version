Privacy Policy

Game Booster does NOT collect, store, or share any personal data.

What we access:

  • Network state - to optimize WiFi for gaming
  • Running apps - to clear RAM (requires user permission)
  • Display overlay - for FPS counter (requires user permission)
  • Notifications - for DND mode (requires user permission)

We DO NOT:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track your location
  • Access your files, contacts, or messages
  • Share data with third parties

All processing happens locally on your device. No data leaves your phone.
